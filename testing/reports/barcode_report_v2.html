This report summarizes the standalone Barcode_estimation_v2 caller. The caller separates barcodes into three zones before final calling:

  • obvious good droplets retained above the upper knee;
  • obvious bad droplets excluded below the low-count threshold; and
  • an ambiguous middle population evaluated with EmptyDrops.

Spliced percentage is not used to make the call, but it is surfaced as a QC diagnostic. For the current run, lower spliced percentages are generally more consistent with nuclear RNA profiles; unusually high spliced fractions among called nuclei are potential concerns.

Barcode rank classification

These plots show the three decision regions directly on the barcode-rank curve. The dashed blue line marks the upper-knee retain threshold, the dashed orange line marks the low-count exclusion threshold, and the dotted vertical line indicates the midpoint used to define the empty plateau.
